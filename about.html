About

I follow the systems beneath the experience.

I started in enterprise content delivery and Adobe AEM operations, where I learned that the quality of an experience often depends on systems customers never see. As my work expanded into personalization, content modeling, analytics, taxonomy, ServiceNow, consulting, team transformation, and AI readiness, I kept following those systems deeper.

The through-line is structure: how information is organized, connected, governed, measured, and sustained. That is where I have found the hardest enterprise problems, and where I do my best work.

How I work

Six principles that shape how I lead the work.

I make complex systems understandable.Structure only works when the people using it can see how the pieces fit together and what to do next.

I build capability, not dependency.If the work only functions while I am in the room, I have not finished the job.

I connect strategy with execution.Strategy has to survive contact with production. I stay close enough to the work to see what holds and what needs to change.

I use evidence to guide decisions.Opinion can start the conversation. Evidence is what helps a team align, prioritize, and move.

I protect quality under pressure.Deadlines matter, but so does the cost of rework. I design controls that help teams move quickly without sacrificing reliability.

I design for sustainment.Ownership, standards, governance, and measurement should keep working after launch and after I leave.
