What came next

The taxonomy solved one problem. The engagement revealed a bigger one.

Across this engagement and others, I saw the same pattern: organizations needed more than a hierarchy. They needed help implementing it, connecting content, training owners, measuring performance, and governing the structure after launch.

I used those recurring gaps to build a more complete enterprise taxonomy consulting model.
